Analyst ratings

What the analysts who cover each company think, averaged together: their buy, hold or sell rating and where they expect the price to be in a year. Useful as a second opinion, but analysts are often wrong and tend to be too upbeat.

Not financial advice. Ratings and price targets are the average of the analysts Yahoo Finance tracks for each company and refresh every weekday after the US close. Analysts can have conflicts of interest, and targets are often revised after the price moves.

Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance.
