Your account

A free account keeps your watchlists and notes, price alerts, paper trading portfolios, saved screens, chart drawings and settings the same on your phone, tablet and computer. There's no email or password: your account is a private key that TopTicker makes for you. Keep it somewhere safe, because it's the only way into your account.

What's kept in sync

  • Watchlists, with your notes and target prices
  • Price alerts (turn on push alerts on each device you want notified on)
  • Paper trading portfolios and their trades
  • Saved screener screens and Compare and Backtest set-ups
  • Chart drawings on the stock lookup, recently viewed stocks, market clocks and calculator inputs

Changes sync when you open a page, every minute while a page is open and when you leave. If the same thing changed on two devices, the newer change wins. Nothing personal is stored: only these items, under a scrambled copy of your key.
