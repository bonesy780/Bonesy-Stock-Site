Technical signals

Stocks that just triggered a chart signal traders watch, found by scanning every stock after each close. Each card says what the signal means. Signals are clues about momentum, not predictions, and they often fail on their own.

Not financial advice. Signals are worked out from daily closing prices after each market's close, and refresh every weekday after the US close. They describe what the price has done and can't tell you what it will do.
