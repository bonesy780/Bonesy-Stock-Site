How this works

  • United States: Zillow's typical home value (ZHVI, middle third of homes), rents (ZORI), homes for sale, new listings, days to pending, price cuts, sale-to-list ratio, market heat index and 12-month forecast, for the 150 biggest metro areas.
  • Canada: Statistics Canada's New Housing Price Index for each city (new homes only; Canada's resale MLS® figures are licensed and can't be republished), CMHC's average two-bedroom rent, and the Bank of Canada's 5-year mortgage rate.
  • States and provinces: Zillow's typical home value and rent for each US state; Statistics Canada's New Housing Price Index and CMHC rents for each province.
  • Mexico: Sociedad Hipotecaria Federal's quarterly house prices by state. SHF only publishes PDF and Excel tables, so these are entered by hand each quarter.
  • Who has the upper hand: a score from −100 (buyer's market) to +100 (seller's market) that averages Zillow's heat index, sale-to-list ratio, share of price cuts, days to pending, supply change and 3-month price trend.
  • Lending rates: the Federal Reserve, Freddie Mac and the US Treasury (through FRED), the Bank of Canada, and the European Central Bank's data portal (policy rates, €STR, Euribor and the MFI interest rate statistics for home and business loans), refreshed with each data update.
  • Investor heat map: each figure is ranked against the other places shown, and the interest score averages those ranks (flipped where a lower figure means more demand, such as days to sell or price-to-income). It shows where demand and money are moving, not a forecast.
  • Property types: REIT returns come from the market data; cap rates, vacancy and rent growth are rounded figures from public market reports, kept by hand with the date they are from.
  • Gross rent yield: a year of typical rent divided by the typical home value, before costs. A rough guide to whether owning pays.

Market figures are monthly or quarterly statistics from public agencies and Zillow Research, not live prices. Buy ideas and market labels are worked out by fixed rules from those figures. They are not advice: talk to a licensed agent, lender or adviser before you buy.

Data: Zillow Research, BIS house prices, Freddie Mac, FRED, Statistics Canada and CMHC, Bank of Canada, Sociedad Hipotecaria Federal. European Central Bank, US Census Bureau, REIT prices from Yahoo Finance. Not financial advice.
