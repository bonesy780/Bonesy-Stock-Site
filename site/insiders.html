Insider buying and selling

When a company's officers, directors or big shareholders trade its stock, they have to report it. Insiders sell for many reasons (taxes, a new house, spreading their money out), but they usually buy for one: they think the price will rise. Several insiders buying the same stock at once, a cluster, is the signal people watch most.

Not financial advice. Trades come from the filings Yahoo Finance collects (mostly US companies' SEC Form 4 filings; coverage for other markets is thin) and refresh every weekday after the US close. Filings can arrive up to two business days after the trade. Values are converted to US dollars.
